Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Pass US Civics ("the App") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data.

Data Collection

The App does not require account creation, login, or any personal information to use. The App does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking of any kind.

Local Storage

The App does not save or persist any user data, progress, or identifiers on your device or on any server.

Device Features

The App uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to read questions and answers aloud. This audio is generated and played entirely on your device and is never recorded, transmitted, or shared.

Third Parties

The App does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
